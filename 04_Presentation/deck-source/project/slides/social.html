Victor (about 1 minute). Third barrier: social licence. In the 2024 Lowy Institute Poll, 61% of Australians supported using nuclear power for electricity and 37% opposed it. So national support exists, but it is not consent from a host community. Perceived versus actual risk: per unit of electricity, nuclear has caused about 0.03 deaths per TWh, including Chernobyl and Fukushima, against about 24.6 for coal, mostly from air pollution (Our World in Data). People fear the accident, not the everyday pollution. Non-technical solutions: real consent from the Hunter community and First Nations, a waste plan agreed before construction, and local benefit sharing.
