Victor (about 1 minute). Second barrier: cost. CSIRO’s GenCost reports (2024–25 and the current 2025–26 edition) rank SMRs as the most expensive new-build option in Australia. That holds even with real Darlington cost data, which landed inside CSIRO’s projected range. Renewables with firming and transmission are cheapest. The US warning is NuScale’s Carbon Free Power Project, planned at Idaho National Laboratory for a group of Utah utilities. Its target power price rose 53%, from $58 to $89 per MWh (2020 and 2022 dollars, so partly inflation), and the $89 already included about $30 per MWh of Inflation Reduction Act credits and Department of Energy support. Too few utilities signed up, and the 462 MW project was cancelled in November 2023. DOE had obligated about $232 million to it. Non-technical solution: someone has to carry the first-of-a-kind risk. In Canada the province and federal funds are taking equity stakes in Darlington.
