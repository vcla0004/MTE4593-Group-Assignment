Victor (about 1 minute). Second barrier: cost. CSIRO’s 2024–25 GenCost report ranks SMRs as the most expensive new-build option in Australia. That holds even with real Darlington cost data, which landed inside CSIRO’s projected range. Renewables with firming and transmission are cheapest. The US warning is NuScale’s project in Utah: its target power price rose from $58 to $89 per MWh, utilities wouldn’t sign up, and the 462 MW project was cancelled in November 2023 after $232 million of Department of Energy funding. Non-technical solution: someone has to carry the first-of-a-kind risk. In Canada the province and federal funds are taking equity stakes in Darlington.
