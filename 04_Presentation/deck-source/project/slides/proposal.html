Leroy (about 1.5 minutes). This is our specific proposal. Location: the Liddell Power Station site in the Hunter Valley, which closed in 2023 and still has a high-voltage grid connection. Scale: one 300 MWe BWRX-300, which at a 90% capacity factor (our assumption) is about 2.4 TWh a year. Application: firm low-carbon power to replace coal in the National Electricity Market. The materials idea is to make the reactor’s concrete with fly ash reclaimed from Liddell’s own ash dams in place of part of the cement. That turns a coal waste into a nuclear construction material on the same site. Sam will now show what that does to the numbers.
