Leroy (about 1 minute). Walk the timeline left to right. OPG applied for its construction licence in October 2022; the regulator granted it in April 2025; in May 2025 Ontario approved construction of Unit 1, within a C$20.9 billion budget for all four units (1,200 MW); Unit 1 is expected in service in October 2030, according to OPG’s Ontario Energy Board filing. Divide C$20.9 billion by 1,200 MW and you get about C$17 million per MW. That makes Darlington the first Western SMR with both a construction licence and a full budget, which is why our analysis uses it. Transition: “Here’s what we propose to do differently.”
