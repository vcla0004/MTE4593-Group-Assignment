Leroy (about 1 minute). Walk the timeline left to right. OPG applied for its construction licence in October 2022; the regulator granted it in April 2025; in May 2025 Ontario approved four units, 1,200 MW in total, for C$20.9 billion; Unit 1 is expected to start commercial operation by the end of 2029. Divide C$20.9 billion by 1,200 MW and you get about C$17 million per MW. That makes Darlington the benchmark for SMR cost and timing in Western countries, which is why our analysis uses it.
