## 6. Method caveats (for §7 and the uncertainty discussion)

1. **Boundary mismatch.** Grid factors (NGA scope 2, TAF, CER) count combustion at the power station only; the SMR value is life cycle. Comparing them understates displacement slightly. The boundary-consistent row (UNECE coal LCA vs SMR LCA) is given as the upper bound.
2. **Average vs marginal.** A new 300 MWe baseload unit displaces whatever is on the margin when it runs, not the average. In Ontario the margin is mostly gas, so the marginal factor is the better short-run estimate; over a 60-year life the margin decarbonises, so the flat marginal case is an upper bound.
3. **Counterfactual.** In both grids the alternative to an SMR is not 'today's grid forever' but other new supply. The same-money comparison (`same_money.md`) addresses this.
4. **Liddell closed in April 2023.** An SMR at the Liddell site would reuse the site and grid connection but displaces the NEM mix, not Liddell. Bayswater intensity is therefore an upper bound only.
